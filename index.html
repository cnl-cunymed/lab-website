Our Mission

Translating discovery into recovery.

The Kim Lab uses advanced neuroimaging and neuropsychological methods to study neurodegeneration and neuroplasticity after traumatic brain injury. We investigate the mechanisms of functional recovery and develop novel interventions — including neuromodulation, psychoactive drugs, and cognitive training. Our long-term aim is to turn discoveries about TBI biomarkers into therapies that meaningfully improve patients' lives.

The science we do.

tPBM for Chronic TBI
