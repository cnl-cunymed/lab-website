2024 – 2028 · U.S. Department of Defense · Principal Investigator

tPBM Phase II Clinical Trial for Chronic TBI in Older Adults

A four-year randomized double-blind clinical trial of transcranial photobiomodulation as a non-invasive therapy for chronic traumatic brain injury, enrolling 80 participants aged 55 and older across NYU Rusk Rehabilitation and the CUNY Advanced Science Research Center.
