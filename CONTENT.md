### Junghoon Kim, PhD — Principal Investigator

- **Title:** Principal Investigator · Assistant Medical Professor
- **Photo:** `images/people/junghoon-kim.jpg` (+ `.webp`)
- **Links:** jkim@med.cuny.edu · [PubMed bibliography](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/myncbi/junghoon.kim.1/bibliography/public/) · [ORCID 0000-0001-5729-8993](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5729-8993) · [CUNY faculty page](https://medicine.cuny.edu/staff/junghoon-kim-phd/)

Dr. Kim leads the Clinical Neuroimaging Lab at the CUNY School of Medicine, where
his work combines advanced neuroimaging and neuropsychological methods to study
neurodegeneration and neuroplasticity after traumatic brain injury. His research
focuses on the mechanisms of functional recovery, the effects of clinical
interventions such as neuromodulation, psychoactive drugs, and cognitive
training, and the development of novel neuroimaging biomarkers of TBI.

Dr. Kim is the Principal Investigator on a Department of Defense grant supporting
a Phase II clinical trial of transcranial photobiomodulation (tPBM) in older
adults with chronic TBI, conducted with NYU Rusk Rehabilitation, the CUNY
Advanced Science Research Center, and The City College of New York.

- **Photo:** `images/people/abubakar-yamin.jpg` (+ `.webp`)
- **Links:** [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K9nF3SsAAAAJ)

Dr. Yamin's research focuses on neuroimaging analysis, brain functional
connectivity, and the application of machine learning to medical imaging,
particularly for disease classification in neurological conditions. He serves as
Co-Investigator on the lab's DOD-funded tPBM clinical trial, where he leads the

### James Barefoot, BA — Master's Student, Cognitive Neuroscience

- **Photo:** `images/people/james-barefoot.jpg` (+ `.webp`) — square crop from the
  head-and-shoulders original he supplied
- **Program:** M.S. Cognitive Neuroscience, The Graduate Center, CUNY
- **Prior degree:** B.A. Psychology
- **Bio supplied by:** James, by email, 2026-08-03

James is a master's student in Cognitive Neuroscience at the CUNY Graduate
Center, completing pre-medical coursework as a post-baccalaureate student
alongside his degree in preparation for medical school.

His thesis tests how reliably brain entropy can be measured from functional MRI
(fMRI) across repeat scans, then compares healthy controls with
moderate-to-severe traumatic brain injury (TBI) patients, both at a single time
point and across recovery. He is also co-authoring a scoping review on endocrine
dysregulation after TBI and the therapeutic potential of transcranial
photobiomodulation (tPBM).

## Publications

The publications page carries **39 entries, every one with Junghoon Kim on the
author list**, spanning 2005 to 2026. It was rebuilt from Dr. Kim's public My
NCBI bibliography reconciled against the PubMed API, not typed by hand.

Rules that were applied, and should keep being applied:

1. **Kim must be an author.** A paper from his bibliography with no Kim on the
   author list was removed.
2. Citations come from PubMed. Dr. Kim's My NCBI bibliography is the list of
   what belongs; PubMed supplies the exact volume, issue, pages, and DOI.
3. His name is wrapped in `<span class="pi">` so it renders in brand teal.
4. `data-year` on each `<li>` must match a year chip, or the filter silently
   drops the entry.

Two records need a human decision, both verified as genuinely his (Moss
Rehabilitation Research Institute affiliation, with Whyte / Hart / Coslett) but
absent from his curated My NCBI list:

- Kim J, Whyte J, Hart T, Vaccaro M, Polansky M, Coslett HB. *Executive function
  as a predictor of inattentive behavior after traumatic brain injury.* J Int
  Neuropsychol Soc. 2005;11(4):434-45. PMID 16209424
- Hart T, Whyte J, Kim J, Vaccaro M. *Executive function and self-awareness of
  "real-world" behavior and attention deficits following traumatic brain injury.*
  J Head Trauma Rehabil. 2005;20(4):333-47. PMID 16030440

One entry is not in PubMed and is maintained by hand from its DOI: Gaggi NL et
al., *Transcranial Photobiomodulation and Chronic Traumatic Brain Injury*,
Photonics 2024;11(3):260, doi:10.3390/photonics11030260.

## What is deliberately NOT in this file

Kept out on purpose, because this repository is shared on GitHub:

- Personal phone numbers
- Personal (non-institutional) email addresses
- Home addresses
- CVs and any document holding the above

James supplied a phone number and a personal Gmail address along with his bio.
Neither is recorded here and neither is on the site. Only institutional email
addresses ever belong on a public lab page, and at present only the PI's is
published.

Two source files are kept locally and excluded from git by `.gitignore`:
`YLi_CV_NIW.pdf` and `Yifei_Headshot.pdf`. They have never been committed. The
raw photographs James sent are likewise excluded; the only version that ships is
the cropped headshot in `images/people/`.
