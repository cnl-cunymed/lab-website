Ways to get involved

Prospective trainees

Interested in joining the lab as a PhD student or postdoctoral researcher? We welcome inquiries from candidates with backgrounds in neuroscience, biomedical engineering, computer science, clinical psychology, or related disciplines. Email a CV and short statement of research interests to Dr. Kim.

Study participants & clinical partners

If you're interested in participating in our research on traumatic brain injury — or if you're a clinician interested in referring patients to ongoing studies — please get in touch. We'd love to hear from you.
